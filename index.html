An open agentic website · multi-agent verification, in your browser

Verify a claim against recent news.

Paste a claim — or a paragraph from an article — and a small network of agents finds the sources, cross-checks them, and writes a sourced verdict with a confidence score. The agents run in your browser on your model key. This site never sees your key and never pays for inference.

    How it works
    
    
      
      
    1. 
        front_desk_coordinator
        
      Takes your claim and runs the verification: source finding, cross-checking, then the verdict.
      
      
      2. 
      
    2. 
        source_finder
        
      Searches the site’s recent-coverage snapshot — headlines refreshed every 6 hours from public news RSS — and returns matching sources with links and dates.
      
      
      3. 
      
    3. 
        cross_checker
        
      Compares the sources: where they agree, where they conflict, whether they are independent outlets, and what is missing.
      
      
      4. 
      
    4. 
        verdict_writer
        
      Writes the verdict as structured data: supported, refuted, mixed, or unverifiable — with evidence, a confidence score, and what would change the verdict.
      
      
      5. 
    
    
    
    After a verdict, you can ask for a citation audit: this site calls the
      Citation Mirror
      peer network, which runs a six-trial spot check on the model that wrote the brief — in your browser,
      on your key, and only after you approve it on a consent card.

    On-device model (experimental)
    
    
    Run a small model locally with WebLLM — no key needed. Requires a browser with
      WebGPU and downloads about 1 GB of model weights on first use. Tool calling on a 1.5B model is
      less reliable than a hosted model; use it to try the site, not for careful verification.
